## Key Topics
- **Service Owner Proposal**: Discussion on defining `service.owner` attributes to address the fragmentation in ownership tags across cloud providers and improve incident response.
- **Operational Ownership**: Agreement that the `owner` attribute should reflect operational ownership, differentiating it from financial or infrastructure ownership.
- **Data Classification Proposal**: Consideration of renaming the data attribute group to "data classification" to clarify its purpose and scope.
- **Feedback and Demos**: Need for demos to illustrate the proposed attributes and gather user feedback on their utility and clarity.

## Action Items

- Explore user feedback on the proposed naming and scope of the data classification attributes.
