## Key Topics
- **Kubernetes Attributes Processor**: Plans for breaking changes and the promotion of internal telemetry feature gate before tagging version 1.0.
- **Prometheus Component Stability**: Discussion on aligning versioning with Prometheus and the implications of declaring stability.
- **Dynamic Telemetry Routing**: Proposal for dynamic routing of telemetry to different consumers and the challenges associated with it.
- **Pipeline Status Logic**: Proposed changes to how pipeline statuses are determined based on component statuses, particularly regarding stopping and error states.
- **Collaboration with Prometheus**: Discussion on a new collector distribution that includes Prometheus exporters and the implications for production readiness.
