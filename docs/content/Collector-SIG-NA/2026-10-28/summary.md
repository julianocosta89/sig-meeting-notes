## Key Topics
- Discussion on the integration of Prometheus exporters with OpenTelemetry Collector and the need for user guidance on component choices.
- Proposal to deprecate Zipkin and Jaeger receivers due to the availability of OTLP ingestion methods, with differing opinions on their necessity.
- Introduction of a feature request regarding gRPC's Shopper Address List option and its potential implementation.
- Ongoing collaboration between Prometheus and OpenTelemetry communities for better integration strategies.
