## Key Topics
- **Weekly meeting logistics:** Sessions are held Wednesdays at the same time on Zoom, recordings are stored automatically, and questions can be posted in the Slack channel at any time.
- **Localization issue and PR workflow:** Contributors open a tracking issue for a section, comment before starting work to claim a page, and open one PR per page to make review easier and avoid monopolizing an issue.
- **Contributor onboarding:** New contributors need to sign the CNCF CLA (individually or through their employer) and disclose any AI tool use. Starting with a short page is recommended.
- **Romanian style rules:** Use the informal "tu" form rather than "dumneavoastră," write glossary and technical terms in lowercase, and add missing words to the Romanian spellcheck dictionary.
- **Review and advancement:** Anyone can review PRs. Consistent contributions (around five to ten PRs) can lead to nomination as an approver or maintainer.
