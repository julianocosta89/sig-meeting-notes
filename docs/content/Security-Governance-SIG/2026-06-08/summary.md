## Key Topics
- Discussion on the increasing volume of AI-generated security reports and the need for an AI-based auto triage system.
- The role of the Security SIG in managing security advisories and the importance of directing reports to individual SIGs.
- Current processes for handling security advisories, including the on-call rotation of the Technical Committee (TC) members.
- The need for clearer guidelines and expectations regarding response times for security vulnerabilities.
- Challenges in balancing security expectations with the volunteer nature of maintainers' contributions.
