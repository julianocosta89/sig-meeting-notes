## Key Topics
- Discussion on the development of a tracer configurator spec to enable/disable instrumentation at runtime.
- Overview of the implementation status of rule-based sampling and its relation to the Java implementation.
- Consideration of the use of underscore-prefixed methods and their potential stability in the SDK.
- Exploration of how experimental features could be managed in the Python SDK, referencing Java's incubating package approach.

## Action Items
- Continue to refine the tracer configurator spec and its implementation.
- Investigate the possibility of adopting a similar approach to Java's incubating package for experimental features.
