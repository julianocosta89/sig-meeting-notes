## Key Topics
- Discussion on the RFC for stabilizing the core collector and contrib components, particularly the host metrics receiver.
- Concerns about the relationship between component stability and semantic conventions, with emphasis on the need for stable semantic conventions before marking components as stable.
- The idea of "grandfathering" the host metrics receiver into stability due to its long-standing use and existing conventions.
- The necessity for clear documentation on the strategy for stabilization and the relationship between components and semantic conventions.
- Future considerations for release cadence and versioning of components in relation to the core distribution.

## Action Items
- Document the strategy for stabilizing the host metrics receiver and its relationship with semantic conventions.
- Create guidelines for marking components as stable and ensure clarity for users and contributors.
- Explore the possibility of stabilizing a subset of semantic conventions required for the resource detection processor and other components.
- Post a summary of the discussion on the RFC and communicate updates via Slack.
