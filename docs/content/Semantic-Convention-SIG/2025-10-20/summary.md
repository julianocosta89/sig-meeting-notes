## Key Topics
- **Issue Triage Process**: Discussion on the new triage board and the need for a structured approach to manage issues and PRs.
- **PProf Compatibility PR**: Review of a PR related to PProf compatibility, emphasizing the need for documentation on mapping and attributes.
- **Peer Service Discussion**: Ongoing debate about the `peer.service` attribute, its implications for OpenTelemetry, and the need for a clear path forward.
- **Call for How-To Guides**: Emphasis on the need for comprehensive how-to guides for stabilizing metrics, spans, and events in OpenTelemetry.
- **Cloud Attributes Group**: Discussion on the need to form a group to standardize cloud resource attributes across different providers.

## Action Items
- **Follow Up on Triage Board**: Review and triage issues during future meetings.
- **Documentation for PProf**: Create documentation outlining the mapping and usage of PProf attributes.
- **Discuss Peer Service Namespace**: Continue discussions among maintainers regarding the `peer.service` attribute and its implications.
- **Create How-To Guides**: Encourage participants to draft how-to guides for OpenTelemetry conventions.
- **Form Cloud Attributes Group**: Initiate the formation of a group to standardize cloud resource attributes.
