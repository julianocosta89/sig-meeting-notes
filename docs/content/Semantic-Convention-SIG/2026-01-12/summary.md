## Key Topics
- **Deprecation of RPC Message Events**: Discussion on removing RPC message events from the semantic conventions due to their limited utility and the preference for including full message content in traces.
- **New SEMCOM Release**: Agreement on the need to cut a new semantic conventions release in January, with a call for contributions from the SIG members.
- **Guidance on OTEL Semantic Conventions**: Discussion on stability and versioning for new semantic conventions, particularly for CICD, and the implications of breaking changes for vendors.
- **Oracle Database Semantic Conventions PR**: Update on a PR regarding modifications to the Oracle database semantic conventions, with a request for review.
