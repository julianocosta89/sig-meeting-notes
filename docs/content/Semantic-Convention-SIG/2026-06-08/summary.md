- **Stability of Entities**: Discussion on stabilizing entities in the OpenTelemetry specification and the implications for semantic conventions.

- **New Release Preparation**: Planning for an upcoming release, including deprecation of GenAI-related items and the need for updated release notes.

- **Software Carbon Intensity Specification**: Introduction of the Green Software Foundation's initiative to integrate carbon intensity metrics into OpenTelemetry.
