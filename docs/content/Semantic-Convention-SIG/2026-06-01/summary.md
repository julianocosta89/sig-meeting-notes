## Key Topics
- Proposal for adding new network attributes to OpenTelemetry, including network peer hostname and address prefix.
- Discussion on the alignment of OpenTelemetry with Elastic Common Schema (ECS) and potential conflicts.
- The importance of defining signals that utilize new attributes to ensure clarity and usability.
- Consideration of making certain attributes opt-in for metrics to manage cardinality concerns.
- Formation of a working group to review and discuss network-related proposals and attributes.
