## Key Topics
- Discussion on the semantic conventions for OpenTelemetry and their application in profiling and instrumentation.
- Clarification on the definition of "service" in the context of OpenTelemetry, especially in distributed systems versus monolithic architectures.
- Updates on the V2 schema for spans and metrics, including the introduction of new attributes and the flattening of groups.
- Exploration of the use of span links and attributes in messaging scenarios, particularly in the context of Elixir and Broadway.
- Challenges and best practices for tagging and naming conventions in telemetry data.
