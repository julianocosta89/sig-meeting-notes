## Key Topics
- Discussion on log levels and the opt-in/opt-out nature of logging in OpenTelemetry.
- Proposal to decentralize the OpenTelemetry configuration schema, allowing Semantic Conventions to own relevant definitions.
- Review of the process for migrating namespaces to the client-side registry.
- Examination of how federated semantic conventions should be structured and displayed on the OpenTelemetry website.
- Exploration of configuration options for enabling/disabling telemetry signals across different types.
