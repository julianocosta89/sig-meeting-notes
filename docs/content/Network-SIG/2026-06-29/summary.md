## Key Topics
- Objectives for the Network SIG, including expanding semantic conventions, defining standard telemetry methods, improving usability of network-related components, and supporting OPI's telemetry functions.
- Discussion on collaboration with the Prometheus community to align SNMP exporters and receivers.
- Review of existing network telemetry entities and attributes, with a focus on data center networking as a priority.
- Considerations regarding the handling of unsigned 64-bit counters in metrics and the implications for reporting deltas versus cumulative values.
