## Key Topics
- Discussion on the creation of a federated semantic conventions project for OpenTelemetry, including the need for a project proposal.
- Review of existing GitHub issues related to the new working group for networking and the challenges of managing discussions within a single ticket.
- Exploration of the terminology used in network attributes, specifically the terms "peer" and "address," and their implications for clarity and standardization.
- The importance of aligning network attributes with other SIGs to avoid fragmentation and ensure consistency in definitions.
- Planning for future meetings and collaboration on project documentation.
