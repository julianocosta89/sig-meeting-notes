## Key Topics
- Discussion on naming conventions for input and output tokens in OpenTelemetry, particularly focusing on the use of "usage" and "detailed."
- Differentiation between counters and histograms for tracking token metrics.
- Consideration of dimensions related to context length (short vs. long) and their implications for billing and metrics.
- Proposal to implement "usage.inputTokens" and "operation.inputTokens" as new metrics.
- Exploration of compute units as a potential additional metric related to token usage.
