## Key Topics
- Discussion on the JavaScript prototype for resource initialization and handling asynchronous attributes.
- Challenges of synchronous vs. asynchronous operations in different programming languages (Java vs. JavaScript).
- Importance of ensuring that the OpenTelemetry specification accommodates all languages without blocking operations.
- Strategies for managing resource detection and conflict resolution in asynchronous environments.

## Action Items

- Ensure that the OpenTelemetry specification is adaptable for both synchronous and asynchronous operations across different languages.
