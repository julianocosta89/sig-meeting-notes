## Key Topics
- Discussion on the challenges of service entity identification in OpenTelemetry, particularly regarding observed and observing services.
- Proposal for introducing a new entity type, "observed services," to clarify relationships and avoid conflating different service instances.
- Review of a pull request (PR) that aims to redefine the concept of "resource" in the OpenTelemetry specification to better represent observed entities.
- Debate on whether the changes in the PR constitute a breaking change in the specification.
