## Key Topics
- Discussion on the concept of identifying vs. descriptive attributes in OpenTelemetry entities and their implications for metrics and time series.
- Clarification on how changes in descriptive attributes affect querying and aggregation in Prometheus.
- Exploration of the relationship between OpenTelemetry entities and Prometheus, emphasizing that entities should not fundamentally change existing behaviors.
- Consideration of how to improve user experience with resource attributes in Prometheus, including potential automatic relabeling based on identifying attributes.
