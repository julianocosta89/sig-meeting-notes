## Key Topics
- Discussion on the SDK specifications, particularly regarding synchronous vs. asynchronous resource detection.
- Challenges with entity detection in JavaScript SDK and potential solutions for handling mutable entities.
- Proposal for a separate browser SDK to accommodate unique browser requirements and simplify implementation.
- The relationship between entities and their identifiers, particularly in the context of processes and containers.
