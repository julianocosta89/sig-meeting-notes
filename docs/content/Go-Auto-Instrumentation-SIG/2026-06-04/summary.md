## Key Topics
- Discussion on the future of the Go Auto project and its potential sunset in favor of the OBI project.
- Concerns about user confusion regarding the overlapping functionalities of Go Auto and OBI.
- Plans for a migration path for users from Go Auto to OBI, including feature parity considerations.
- The importance of clear communication to the community regarding the deprecation of Go Auto.
- The need for a coordinated effort to ensure that the OBI project can adequately support users transitioning from Go Auto.

## Action Items
- Draft a communication document for the GC outlining the decision to sunset Go Auto and its implications.
- Notify the community about the deprecation of Go Auto and direct them to OBI for future needs.
- Assess the Auto SDK's functionality and determine where it should reside post-sunset.
- Identify any gaps in OBI compared to Go Auto and create tasks to address them.
