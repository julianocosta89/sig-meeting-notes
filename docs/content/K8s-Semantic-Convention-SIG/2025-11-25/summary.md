## Key Topics
- Discussion on Kubernetes attributes required by the OpenTelemetry collector, focusing on specific processors and receivers.
- Consideration of guidelines for covering custom Kubernetes resources in semantic conventions.
- The role of Weaver in defining and validating custom registries for telemetry.
- Entity relationships in Kubernetes and how they should be handled within the semantic conventions.
