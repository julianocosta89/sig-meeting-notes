## Key Topics
- Promotion of page fault and file system metrics, with discussions on naming conventions and alignment with Kubernetes namespaces.
- Utilization metrics for pods and nodes, including proposals for deprecating certain metrics and defining new ones.
- Discussion on volume-related metrics and their relationship with persistent volume claims.
- Clarification of metrics for local path volumes and potential issues with reporting disk capacity.
- Ongoing discussions about process metrics and naming conventions within the OpenTelemetry context.

## Action Items
- Propose deprecation of certain node utilization metrics and finalize definitions for pod utilization metrics.
- Further investigate volume-related metrics and their accuracy in reporting.
- Review and clarify naming conventions for process metrics in the context of OpenTelemetry.
