## Key Topics
- Discussion on Kotlin Multiplatform donation and its implications for OpenTelemetry.
- Concerns regarding the stability of various components in OpenTelemetry and the need for a clear definition of "stability."
- The importance of tracking stabilization efforts across different SIGs and ensuring compliance with specifications.
- The need for a process to categorize and prioritize stabilization efforts for the OpenTelemetry specification.
- The role of user feedback in determining the importance of features and stability in the OpenTelemetry ecosystem.
