## Key Topics
- Discussion on the "Stable by Default" OTEP and its implications for OpenTelemetry components.
- Concerns about the effectiveness of changing defaults to prevent breaking changes in production deployments.
- The need for dividing the OTEP into smaller work streams to facilitate quicker progress.
- Governance of OpenTelemetry distributions and the challenges of enforcing stability across various implementations.
- The impact of semantic conventions on the stability of instrumentation libraries.

## Action Items
- Consider dividing the "Stable by Default" OTEP into multiple focused OTEPs to address specific issues.
- Form a group to manage distribution release and stability concerns.
- Further discussion on governance and enforcement mechanisms for OpenTelemetry distributions.
