## Key Topics
- Release of Version 2 of the Open Metrics standard and its implementation by Prometheus.
- Discussion on the decoupling of responsibilities in environment variable propagation and approval of related OTEPs.
- Proposal for integrating OpenTelemetry APIs into language runtimes, with plans to collaborate with the .NET Runtime Team for insights.
- Examination of a product proposal regarding a cohesive installation process for OpenTelemetry, emphasizing the need for TC involvement and the importance of stability.
- Addressing the increase in security advisories and establishing a standard operating procedure for handling them.
