## Key Topics
- Discussion on the need for a comprehensive security maintenance approach for OpenTelemetry.
- Proposal for a new role of Spec Maintainer to ensure domain expertise is recognized and utilized.
- Debate on whether Technical Committee (TC) members should also be Spec Maintainers, with arguments presented for both sides.
- Importance of maintaining a balance between TC responsibilities and the evolving needs of the OpenTelemetry ecosystem.
- Plans to formalize the roles of maintainers across different repositories to ensure consistency.
