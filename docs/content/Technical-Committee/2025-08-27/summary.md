## Key Topics
- **OpenTelemetry PHP Due Diligence**: Discussion on the zero-code instrumentation for PHP, including its architecture and packaging.
- **Instrumentation Mechanics**: Clarification on the process of enabling zero-code instrumentation and its differences from other approaches.
- **Integration with Existing Systems**: Need for collaboration with PHP maintainers to ensure compatibility and support for existing instrumentation.
- **Acceptance Requirements**: Establishing a plan for transitioning from the current instrumentation to the new solution.

## Action Items
- Work with PHP maintainers to define what zero-code instrumentation should look like.
- Develop a plan for phasing out existing zero-code instrumentation in favor of the new implementation.
- Ensure alignment between resource detection in both native and PHP components.
