## Key Topics
- Discussion on the OpenTelemetry mainframe survey results, highlighting a preference for metrics over traces among respondents.
- The potential development of a COBOL SDK and its anticipated limited adoption, primarily among financial sector customers.
- Insights on the primary industries using OpenTelemetry, with a focus on financial services and insurance.
- The importance of OpenTelemetry in maintaining the relevance of mainframes in modern IT environments.
- The need for a callable interface for COBOL applications to integrate with OpenTelemetry.

## Action Items
- Follow up on the development of the COBOL SDK and gauge interest among potential users.
- Explore ways to promote the adoption of OpenTelemetry in mainframe environments to enhance their relevance.
