## Key Topics
- Discussion on denoting unstable properties and types in OpenTelemetry's declarative config, including the use of suffixes and potential alternatives for stability representation.
- Review of the ongoing rollout of security bots and scorecards across OpenTelemetry repositories.
- Consideration of a PR to allow SDKs to expand service name detection, with discussions on its implications for stable behaviors in the spec.
- Debate on the necessity of a global propagator in Kotlin, with insights into the experiences from other languages and the potential for a more flexible design.
