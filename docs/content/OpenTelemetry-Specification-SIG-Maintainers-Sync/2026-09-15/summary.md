## Key Topics
- **Attribute Collection Representation**: Discussion on stabilizing the attribute collection representation for non-OTL protocols, with prototypes needed for multiple languages.
- **Limits on Metrics and Resource Attributes**: Debate on whether to implement default limits for metrics and resource attributes to enhance security and prevent issues.
- **Registry Maintenance Challenges**: Concerns about the maintenance of the registry and the Ecosystem Explorer, with discussions on potential retirement or restructuring.
- **Auto-Instrumentation Requirements**: Proposal for normative requirements to ensure auto-instrumentation does not crash applications, emphasizing the importance of reliability.
- **ITIOps Unified Telemetry Schema Proposal**: Introduction of a draft proposal for operational classification within OCSF, seeking feedback from the OpenTelemetry community.
