## Key Topics
- **Environmental Variable Context Propagation Carrier Document**: Discussion on the status of the PR and its implementations, with plans to merge after the next SIG meeting.
- **Security Vulnerability Reporting Model**: Debate on the definitions of security vulnerabilities, particularly denial of service, and the implications of authenticated client actions.
- **Injector Tool and Injectable Distribution Contracts**: Introduction of a new contract model for specifying installation prerequisites and responsibilities between injector tools and injectable distributions.
- **Security Advisory Process Improvements**: Discussion on the need for clearer guidelines and responsibilities for maintainers regarding security advisories and the urgency of addressing publicly known vulnerabilities.
- **RPC Semantic Conventions Stability**: Announcement that RPC semantic conventions are being marked stable, with a call for final reviews.
