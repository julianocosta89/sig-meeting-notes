## Key Topics
- **Post-Merge Survey**: Discussion on the implementation and feedback from a new survey aimed at new contributors, highlighting positive experiences and areas for improvement.
- **Project Proposals**: Review of four open project proposals, particularly focusing on the MCP and system packaging SIGs, with discussions on the need for TC sponsorship and cross-SIG collaboration.
- **MCP Discussion**: Concerns about the fragmentation of MCP servers and the need for a cohesive approach to MCP development within OpenTelemetry.
- **Zig Language SIG**: Exploration of forming a new SIG for the Zig programming language, emphasizing the importance of staffing and community involvement.
- **Stability and Packaging**: Emphasis on the need for stability in OpenTelemetry components and the role of packaging in ensuring a stable user experience.
