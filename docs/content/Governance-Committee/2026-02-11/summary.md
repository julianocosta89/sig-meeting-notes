## Key Topics
- **Post-Merge Survey:** Discussion on the implementation and feedback from the new post-merge survey aimed at non-members, highlighting positive experiences and areas for improvement.
- **Project Proposals:** Review of four open project proposals, including MCP and system packaging, with emphasis on the need for TC sponsorship and engagement from language SIGs.
- **System Packaging SIG:** Importance of defining project-level policies and ensuring cross-SIG collaboration to address stability and compatibility issues.
- **Contributor Experience:** Consideration of expanding feedback mechanisms to include existing members and specific repository insights.

## Action Items
- Explore the possibility of expanding the post-merge survey to include feedback from existing contributors.
- Identify TC sponsors for the system packaging SIG and clarify engagement expectations.
- Discuss the creation of cross-project guidance mechanisms to address stability and policy alignment across SIGs.
