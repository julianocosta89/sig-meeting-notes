## Key Topics
- **Website Redesign**: Discussion on updating the OpenTelemetry landing page and soliciting feedback on the new design.
- **Stable by Default OTEP**: Review of the OTEP regarding stability and production readiness, with emphasis on the need for clear definitions and communication.
- **Security and Accountability**: Concerns raised about maintainers' responsibilities regarding security vulnerabilities and the need for a structured approach to managing security issues.
- **SIG Collaboration**: The need for better coordination and prioritization among Special Interest Groups (SIGs) to ensure quality and stability across projects.
- **Roadmap Development**: Agreement to create a roadmap with defined workstreams and ownership to address key issues identified in the OTEP discussions.
