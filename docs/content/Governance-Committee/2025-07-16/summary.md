## Key Topics
- Triage Process: Discussion on the effectiveness of triage meetings and potential restructuring to alternate between EU and US sessions.
- Governance Committee Elections: Planning for upcoming elections, including timelines and responsibilities.
- Spec Triage Responsibility: Debate on whether spec triage should be handled by spec sponsors and maintainers rather than the Governance Committee.
- Project Management for SIGs: Clarification on project proposal processes for existing SIGs and how they can communicate their work effectively.
- Recording Policy: Discussion on the implications of recording meetings and the presence of third-party tools.
