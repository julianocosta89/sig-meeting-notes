## Key Topics
- **Liaison Channels**: Discussion on including TC sponsors in liaison channels for better visibility and communication.
- **Open Inference Donation**: Approval process for the donation of instrumentation code from Open Inference, with emphasis on maintaining due diligence and community support.
- **Maintainer Incentives**: Exploration of ways to incentivize maintainers, including potential surveys and discussions on their motivations and contributions.
- **End User Engagement**: Strategies for engaging end users in the project and encouraging them to become maintainers.
- **Roadmap Development**: Discussion on the need for a more representative roadmap that reflects the priorities of maintainers and the community.
