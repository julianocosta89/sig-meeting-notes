## Key Topics
- **OTel Graduation and Next Steps**: Discussion on the recent graduation of OpenTelemetry and the need for actionable roadmaps and community involvement.
- **Maintainer Incentives**: Proposal for creating incentives for maintainers to encourage participation and reduce burnout, including voting privileges and project proposal ownership.
- **Security Document Updates**: Discussion on updating the security documentation to better guide maintainers on handling vulnerabilities.
- **TC Membership Changes**: Announcement of changes in Technical Committee (TC) membership, including Josh Suereth's planned resignation and discussions on expanding the TC.
- **Project Management and Follow-ups**: Concerns raised about the lack of follow-up on action items from previous meetings and the need for better tracking and accountability.
