## Key Topics
- Discussion on the Opleano project, focusing on OpenTelemetry Conform APIs for mainframe applications.
- Consideration of performance implications related to the ZOE cross-memory service for telemetry data injection.
- Exploration of the need for a curated list of signals for better interoperability among vendors.
- Challenges in managing diverse telemetry formats from different vendors and the importance of standardization.
- The necessity of defining both the ingestion layer (SDK for COBOL) and the emission layer for telemetry data.
