- **Contributor Survey Update**: Marylia Gutierrez shared results from the recent contributor survey, highlighting positive feedback on responsiveness and helpfulness of maintainers, while also noting areas for improvement regarding review times and clarity on design expectations.
- **Kubernetes Attributes Processor**: Pablo Baeyens announced that the Kubernetes Attributes Processor is ready to be marked stable, pending final feedback.

- **Configurable Retriable Status Codes**: Evan Bradley discussed the potential for making retriable status codes in the OTLP HTTP exporter configurable, with support from participants for this change.
- **Phase One Partial Reload Review**: Blake Rouse requested reviews for the phase one implementation of partial reload, discussing issues encountered and improvements made.
