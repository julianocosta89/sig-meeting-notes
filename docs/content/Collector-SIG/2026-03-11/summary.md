## Key Topics
- Discussion on the RFC for partial reloading of the OpenTelemetry Collector, focusing on its benefits and implementation.
- Proposal to elevate Windows AMD and ARM support from Tier 2 to Tier 1 due to increased contributions and stability.
- Challenges with shared components in status reporting and the need for potential redesign to improve functionality.
- Overview of the current state of health check extensions (V1 and V2) and plans for future development.

## Action Items
- Participants are encouraged to review the RFC for partial reloading and provide feedback.
- A consensus is sought on moving Windows support to Tier 1; participants should express their support or concerns within a week.
- Further investigation into the shared component issues and their impact on status reporting is needed.
