## Key Topics
- Discussion on usability issues related to dead nodes in the OpenTelemetry pipeline and the need for warnings instead of fatal errors.
- Triage of new issues, including a proposal for a configuration model for the OTAP DF engine, focusing on directed acyclic graphs (DAG) and hyperedges.
- Debate over logging styles and event naming conventions, with a focus on how to structure log events.
- Exploration of extension mechanisms for authentication and other functionalities within the engine, including considerations for shared vs. local implementations.
