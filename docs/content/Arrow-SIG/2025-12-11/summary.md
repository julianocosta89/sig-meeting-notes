## Key Topics
- **OpenTelemetry Arrow Promotion**: Discussion on making OpenTelemetry Arrow an official alternative protocol within OpenTelemetry after three years of incubation.
- **Collector Definition and Interoperability**: Examination of the concept of a "collector" in the context of Rust and Go, and the need for interoperability between the two.
- **Performance Benchmarking**: Insights into the performance advantages of using Rust pipelines over Go, with ongoing efforts to establish concrete benchmarks.
- **Internal Logging Improvements**: Proposal for adding internal logging capabilities to aid debugging and performance tracking in Rust implementations.

## Action Items
- Review and discuss the proposal for internal logging in Rust.
- Explore the feasibility of making OpenTelemetry Arrow an officially supported protocol.
- Continue benchmarking efforts between Go and Rust pipelines to quantify performance differences.
