## Key Topics
- Discussion on metrics for input and output tokens, including the need for breakdowns by modality and cache status.
- Consideration of using counters versus histograms for metrics and the implications of each approach.
- The challenge of maintaining clarity and usability in metric naming and aggregation rules, especially with overlapping dimensions.
- Exploration of potential solutions for aggregating token usage across different models and providers.
- The idea of a "mega counter" to simplify metrics while addressing the complexity of overlapping token counts.
