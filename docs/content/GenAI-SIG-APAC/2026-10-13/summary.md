## Key Topics
- Introduction of a new sub-project focused on standardizing metrics for model serving in Kubernetes.
- Discussion on the differences in metrics semantics across various inference engines (e.g., VLLM, TensorRT, SGLang).
- Proposal for a sidecar solution to unify metrics reporting using OpenTelemetry conventions.
- Collaboration opportunities between different stakeholders (e.g., NVIDIA, Alibaba Cloud, LLMD) to establish a common standard.
- The potential for creating a vendor-neutral home for these metrics outside of OpenTelemetry's core repository.

## Action Items
- Establish a community call for the new sub-project to discuss metrics and collaboration.
- Engage with stakeholders from Alibaba Cloud and LLMD to align on metric definitions and standards.
- Explore the possibility of developing a new repository for faster iteration on semantic conventions before merging into the GenAI repo.
