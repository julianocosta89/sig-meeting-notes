## Key Topics
- Discussion on HTTP2 gRPC context propagation and challenges related to trace parent extraction.
- Proposal to extend the OTEL demo with non-instrumented applications and the integration of various instrumentation techniques.

- Considerations regarding the use of eBPF and ProWrite user permissions in logging and instrumentation.
