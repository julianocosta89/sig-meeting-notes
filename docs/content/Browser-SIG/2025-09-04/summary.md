## Key Topics
- Membership in GitHub Organization: Discussion on the process for new members to join the GitHub organization, including requirements for sponsorship and signing the CLA.
- Semantic Conventions for Page View: Debate on naming conventions for the page view event, with suggestions to change from "page_view" to "page.view" for consistency.
- Page Title Attribute: Discussion on the necessity and implications of capturing the page title, with a consensus to exclude it due to potential PII concerns and high cardinality.
- Navigation Type Attribute: Examination of the navigation type attribute and its definitions, with suggestions to clarify its purpose and possibly rename it for better understanding.

## Action Items

- Martin to remove the page title attribute from the semantic conventions for now.
- Further discussion needed on the navigation type attribute and its potential renaming.
