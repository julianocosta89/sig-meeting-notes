## Key Topics
- Discussion on the use of metrics in OpenTelemetry client instrumentation.
- Ergonomics and simplicity in instrumentation setups for application developers.
- The need for guidelines on emitting metrics and handling high cardinality.
- Potential for a processor in the collector to convert events into metrics.
- Recommendations for best practices regarding metrics in end-user-facing applications.

## Action Items
- Draft guidelines for emitting metrics in client SDKs, including when to avoid them.
- Explore the development of a processor in the collector that can generate metrics from events.
