## Key Topics
- Introduction of new participants and overview of the Client Instrumentation SIG's focus on cross-platform topics.
- Discussion on the merging of the crash semantic convention and the proposal for a new attribute to denote telemetry from different app instances.
- Debate on the naming of the proposed attribute (initially "async") and its implications for telemetry reporting.
- Exploration of the challenges in associating telemetry with the correct resource information, especially in the context of crashes and tombstones.
- Consideration of how similar attributes could be useful for web applications, despite differences in how crashes are defined across platforms.
