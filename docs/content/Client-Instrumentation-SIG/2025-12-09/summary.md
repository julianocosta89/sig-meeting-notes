## Key Topics
- Discussion on the relationship between the Client Instrumentation SIG and the Android SIG, emphasizing the need for specific Android updates.
- Exploration of the concept of "breadcrumbs" in telemetry, allowing differentiation between multiple visits to the same screen.
- Consideration of how to implement screen identifiers and navigation IDs across different platforms (Android, Browser).
- Challenges in defining a "screen" in modern UI frameworks like Android's Compose and React.

## Action Items
- Investigate the adoption of the screen ID and breadcrumb concepts in both the Android and Browser SDKs.
- Clarify the semantic conventions for differentiating between multiple instances of screens or tabs in telemetry.
