## Key Topics
- Discussion on the relationship between the Client Instrumentation SIG and the Android SIG, emphasizing the need for platform-specific meetings.
- Exploration of the concept of "breadcrumbs" in telemetry to differentiate user actions across screens and sessions.
- Deliberation on how to handle multiple instances of screens or tabs in different environments (web vs. mobile).
- The challenge of defining what constitutes a "screen" in various contexts and how that impacts telemetry data.
