## Key Topics
- Discussion on the need for consistent mechanisms across signals, including opt-in metric advisory parameters.
- Conversations about adding support for distribution config in the SDK and its potential security concerns.
- Exploration of SEMCOM schemas and the desire for flexibility in telemetry conventions.
- Progress on adding completion methods for synchronous instruments and the push for specification stability to move OpenTelemetry out of incubating status.
- Review of contributions to the instrumentation library and the strategy to encourage library maintainers to integrate OpenTelemetry support directly.
