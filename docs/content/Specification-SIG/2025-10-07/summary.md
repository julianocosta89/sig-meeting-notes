## Key Topics
- Proposal for a "partial trace connector" to improve visibility for long-running jobs in OpenTelemetry.
- Discussion on the challenges of handling in-flight spans and observability during process crashes.
- Exploration of semantic events for span lifecycle management as a potential solution.
- The need for a long-term strategy for handling late-arriving data across spans and metrics.
- Differentiation between solutions for long-running jobs and crash-related data loss.
