## Key Topics
- **Security Advisories Update**: Discussion on the increase in security advisories and the need for maintainers to monitor them actively.
- **Vulnerability Management**: Emphasis on the importance of preemptive scanning for vulnerabilities and the responsibilities of maintainers.
- **CVE Handling**: Clarification on the rotation of responsibilities for handling CVEs and the need for a public document detailing these assignments.
- **AI Tools for Security**: Exploration of using AI tools for vulnerability scanning and sharing strategies for effective usage.
