## Key Topics
- **Maintainer Responsibilities**: Updates on security advisory responsibilities and support for maintainers.
- **General Availability Roadmap**: Discussion on renaming "stable by default" to "general availability" and the implications of this terminology.
- **Deprecation of Components**: Consideration of deprecating unused components like the OpenCensus compatibility document and shims.
- **Self-Observability in SDKs**: Efforts to ensure all SDKs emit self-observability metrics and the importance of setting a good example.
- **Batching Behavior in Collector and SDK**: Project to unify batching behavior and address configuration challenges.

## Action Items
- **Renaming Initiative**: Explore alternative terms for "general availability" to avoid confusion.
- **Deprecation Tracking**: Open a tracking issue for the deprecation of unused components.
- **Guidance Documentation**: Create supplementary guidelines for maintainers regarding self-observability and batching configuration.
- **Project Proposal Review**: Finalize and review the project proposal for batching unification.
