## Key Topics
- Discussion on the OpenTelemetry graduation process and the need for cleanup and stabilization.
- Introduction of the "Snowtel" initiative aimed at improving the installation experience and overall stability.
- Update on the W3C Level 2 implementation, specifically regarding the randomness flag and the need for OpenTelemetry to adopt it to help stabilize the spec.
- The importance of maintainers' feedback and the coordination of efforts to implement necessary features across SDKs.

## Action Items
- Maintainers are encouraged to read the blog post regarding OpenTelemetry's graduation and provide feedback.
- OpenTelemetry should move forward with implementing the W3C Level 2 spec, particularly the randomness flag, and open tickets for each SDK to ensure compliance.
- Further discussions are needed to clarify the stability of the relevant parts of the spec before proceeding.
