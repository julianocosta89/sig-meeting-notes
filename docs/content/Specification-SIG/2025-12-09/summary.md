## Key Topics
- Discussion on reopening an old OTEP related to telemetry attributes for logical services.
- Proposal of two options for marking telemetry: using tracer attributes or context attributes.
- Consideration of automatic inclusion of attributes in telemetry by SDKs and the introduction of built-in processors.
- The importance of user configurability regarding how attributes are recorded in telemetry.
- Comparison to OpenCensus features and potential interactions with baggage.

## Action Items
- Evaluate the feasibility of implementing built-in processors for context attributes in SDKs.
- Clarify user configurability options for attribute inclusion in telemetry.
- Gather feedback on the proposed options for marking telemetry.
