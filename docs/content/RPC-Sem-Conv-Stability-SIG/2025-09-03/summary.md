## Key Topics
- Discussion on the challenges and features of Zoom meetings, particularly regarding recording consent and participant visibility.
- Review of the project board to triage work items related to RPC and semantic conventions.
- Proposal to revise existing instrumentation across languages to identify missing frameworks in semantic conventions.
- Exploration of the need for a unified message transmission namespace to consolidate similar attributes across RPC, HTTP, and messaging.
- Consideration of transport types and the distinction between unary and streaming RPC calls.
