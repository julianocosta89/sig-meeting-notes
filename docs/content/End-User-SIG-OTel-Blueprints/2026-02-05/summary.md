## Key Topics
- Updates from OTel Unplugged, focusing on the discussion of OTel Blueprints and their scope.
- The three proposed blueprints: centralized telemetry platform for Kubernetes, Kubernetes-specific observability, and legacy/non-Kubernetes workflows.
- The relationship between OTel Blueprints and reference architectures, emphasizing that blueprints are living documents while reference architectures are static.
- The need for guidelines and a process for generating and approving blueprint proposals.
- Discussion on the audience for blueprints and the importance of defining who will implement them.
