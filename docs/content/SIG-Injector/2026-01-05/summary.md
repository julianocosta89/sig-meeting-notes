## Key Topics
- Discussion on automating the release flow for the OpenTelemetry Injector and improving GitHub Actions.
- The need for better integration and collaboration between the Injector and Obi, including addressing installation stories and avoiding double instrumentation.
- Exploration of packaging strategies for the Injector, including the potential for a broader OpenTelemetry RPM package that includes various SDKs and the collector.
- The importance of a coherent installation experience for users and the cultural shift needed to promote this within the community.
