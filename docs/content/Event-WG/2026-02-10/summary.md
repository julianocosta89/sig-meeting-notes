## Key Topics
- Discussion on the use of event body and attributes, emphasizing the need for attributes to handle large data.
- Proposal to formalize severity levels for logging and error handling, including the distinction between error and warning levels.
- Exploration of how to handle exceptions and their attributes in relation to spans, including sampling considerations.
- Consideration of internal spans and their logging behavior, particularly in relation to client and server spans.
- Discussion on the potential for a declarative configuration scheme and the need for feedback on experimental features.
