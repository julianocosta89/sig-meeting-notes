## Key Topics
- Discussion on multi-registry manifests and the need for consistency in naming conventions.
- Proposed migration from `registry_manifest` to `manifest` for clarity and simplicity.
- Introduction of the term "Weaver URI" for referencing various resources, with contention over its naming and usage.
- Principles for dependency resolution and schema referencing were outlined, emphasizing the need for unique identifiers.
- Concerns raised about the schema URL format and its implications for external use beyond OpenTelemetry.
